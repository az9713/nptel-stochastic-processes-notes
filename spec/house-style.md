# House style

Use the NPTEL nano-photonics project's graduate textbook depth and the Stanford
CS229 edition's independent mathematical exposition. A chapter opens with a
consequential calculation or modelling decision, develops one recurring case,
and returns to it after deriving the central results.

1. Define terms, symbols, domains, units, initial conditions, and boundary
   conditions before use. Keep a global notation contract and local definitions.
2. State a result precisely, show the decisive derivation, interpret it, and
   check a limiting or numerical case. Proof, citation, heuristic, and numerical
   evidence have different labels. Never turn a simulation into a proof.
3. Use connected textbook prose: one intellectual move per paragraph, concrete
   referents, US English, serial comma, active voice where the actor matters.
   Use “we” sparingly for reader-and-author calculations.
4. Place timestamp links at major section headings only after verifying the
   interval. Source notes distinguish lecture material, researched additions,
   and original examples. Avoid narration of the production process in chapters.
5. Use column probability vectors. Generator columns sum to zero. State angular
   frequency and two-sided spectrum conventions before any spectral formula.
   Distinguish drift, noise amplitude, and diffusion coefficient explicitly.
6. Code follows the mathematical model. State inputs, outputs, side effects,
   assumptions, and failure cases. Print actual values before plotting them.
   Reproduce all figures from retained generators; label synthetic inputs.
7. Figures reveal a mechanism, parameter dependence, or failure. Captions explain
   the inference, with axes and units. Supply alt text and legible phone variants
   when necessary. Inspect a representative figure before scaling its style.
8. Include fully worked solutions, not answer keys. Recompute them independently
   during review. Use code-copy controls and accessible expandable solutions.
9. Cross-references name exact sections/results/figures. Cite verified primary
   sources beside their supported claims. No invented page numbers or quotations.
10. Avoid equations without interpretation, unsupported “obvious” steps, hype,
    ornamental connections, and padding to match a word count.
