# Sources and asset attribution

This independent study edition follows V. Balakrishnan's *Physical Applications
of Stochastic Processes*, IIT Madras, NPTEL115106089. The recording inventory,
chapter timestamp links and local source sections identify lecture material and
researched additions. The instructor's written exposition covers lectures1–11.

The chapter exposition, examples, Python laboratories, computational figures and
worked exercises were authored for this edition. Figures are reproduced by the
retained scripts in `figures/`; numerical inputs and model limits are stated in
the chapters. They are teaching constructions rather than empirical measurements.

The reader bundles MathJax, including its third-party notices, under the retained
[MathJax license](assets/mathjax-LICENSE). Original recordings, captions, slides
and research papers retain their owners' rights. Raw source copies are excluded.
This repository does not relicense those materials or imply institutional
endorsement. The preview image is a screenshot of this edition's reader.
